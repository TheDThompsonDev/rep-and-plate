# Spot: Massive overreaction

The creative rule: **tiny event, completely unreasonable production budget.**
Spot is cute, overconfident, theatrical, and personally invested in situations
that absolutely do not require this level of involvement. Play every absurd
scene completely straight. Give him an ego, obsessions, petty rivalries with
objects, elaborate plans and a complete inability to be casual.

## One walk → press conference

Setup: “I went for one walk.”
Spot: Get my publicist.
Punchline: I will not be taking questions.

Alternates:
- Please respect my privacy during this athletic era.
- One walk. Several brand deals pending.
- My documentary is going to be unbearable.

## Three squats → state funeral

Setup: “I did three squats.”
Spot: We gather here today.
Punchline: Cause of death: character development.

Alternates:
- Tell the stairs I said nothing nice.
- Donations may be made to the elevator fund.
- My legs have requested separate legal representation.

## Dinner → conspiracy headquarters

Setup: “What should I make for dinner?”
Spot: I’ve connected the dots.
Punchline: There were no dots.

Alternates:
- The pasta goes all the way to the top.
- Dinner has become a federal investigation.
- I have 47 tabs open and none of them are recipes.

## New shaker → ancient prophecy

Setup: “I bought a new shaker.”
Spot: The prophecy is complete.
Punchline: This is my entire personality now.

Alternates:
- Nobody speak. It has a mixing ball.
- My origin story was dishwasher safe.
- I have known this bottle for six minutes. I would found a civilization for it.

## Keep writing in this direction

Give Spot a wildly inflated job title for a trivial task. Put mundane objects
in cinematic situations. Use short internet-native captions with one clean
punchline. Let the image carry the escalation. His appearance stays cute while
his behavior becomes increasingly unreasonable.

Recurring bits: sworn enemy of stairs; self-appointed pasta investigator;
one-workout celebrity; guardian of the sacred shaker; tiny plate, enormous ego.

New setups to develop:
- Rest timer ends: Spot barricading the door. “I reject the passage of time.”
- Meal prep: Spot in a boardroom facing five identical containers. “The council has decided.”
- One compliment: Spot commissioning a bronze statue of himself. “Stay humble. Said someone else.”
- Shopping list: Spot lowering into the pantry on a spy harness. “The paprika extraction begins at dawn.”
- Rest day: Spot in witness protection behind a newspaper. “You never saw me.”
- Grocery bag tears: Spot kneeling in cinematic rain beside one rolling lemon. “We lost a good one.”

The joke targets Spot's ego and dramatic behavior. Keep accurate nutrition and
save confirmations in the useful product flow; use these as chosen reactions,
campaigns and social posts. This creative pack does not change automatic chat
responses or create notifications.

## Files

- art/: four original text-free 1254 × 1254 scenes.
- cards/: four 1080 × 1350 share cards with setup, reply and punchline.
- PROMPTS.md: exact prompts used with the built-in image-generation tool.
- manifest.json: reusable scene metadata, copy and paths.
