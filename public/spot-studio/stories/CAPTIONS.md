# Little stories. Big Spot energy.

Six complete scenes for posts, campaign creative and user-chosen chat reactions.
The text-free art is 1254 × 1254. Share cards are 1080 × 1350 (4:5 portrait).
The editable HTML keeps setup, reply and punchline separate from the artwork.

## I’m choosing trust.
- Setup: “I ate half the cheesecake.”
- Spot: Half… got it.
- Punchline: I’m choosing trust.
- Alternate: A very flexible definition of half.
- Use: Self-captioned humor, matching the supplied reference. Let people choose this joke about their own story; don't automatically question their food logs.

## Using the collection

Choose a situation, keep the setup short, and let Spot's expression deliver the joke.
Use the text-free scene for alternate captions, crops and campaign layouts.
Keep jokes out of measurement, estimates and save confirmations: these cards do
not claim a meal was saved or assign nutritional values. Any future app insertion
should use the existing reviewed logging flow and the illustration preference.

The six scenes were created with the built-in image-generation tool from the
user-supplied ChatGPT example. Exact prompts are in PROMPTS.md. Existing sticker
packs, navigation and automatic app responses are unchanged by this collection.
